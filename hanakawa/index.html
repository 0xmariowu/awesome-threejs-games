A river journey

Hanakawa花川

Unrolling the map

Spring mist on the water, the far bank half remembered.

W and S set the throttle. A and D steer.

Four bridges cross the river between the weir and the falls.

Slow down near a landing and press E to dock.

Thirty vermilion torii climb the hill to the shrine.

M opens the river chart and your objectives.

The current carries petals downstream, and it will carry you.

C changes the camera: follow, helm, photo.
